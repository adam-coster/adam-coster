# How we prevent spoilers in demo builds of Crashlands 2

Video game success is all about visibility *in stores*, and Steam is by far the best in the business at surfacing games to people. But it's a scale problem that gets harder year by year: the more games there are on the store, the harder it becomes to surface each individual game to the people who would like it.

So Steam is always introducing new mechanisms to get games in front of people. One of those is "Steam Next Fest", an event that happens a few times a year only for games that have not launched yet, but that do have a demo build. While it's hard to find evidence, in either direction, for demos being a worthwhile marketing tool, it's *always* useful to have more visibility on Steam. So we figured it was worth the (enormous) cost of cutting a demo so we could participate.

While there were many challenges related to making and releasing a demo of [Crashlands 2](https://crashlands2.com), the one I'm focusing on here was: how do make demo builds in a way that doesn't expose secrets and work-in-progress to players, in a way that will allow us to make new demo versions from the main project whenever we want?
