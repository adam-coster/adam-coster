# Video Games Cannot Benefit from Generative AI

We've been hearing for a few years now how "transformative" generative AI, LLMs in particular, are going to be for the video games industry. We have yet to see a single compelling use case, however. While AI advocates explain this away as being just around the corner, once the models get smaller, or once some game designer just gets *smart enough* to figure out how to use it... generative AI is fundamentally at odds with what video games are all about: *designed constraints and predictable outcomes*.
